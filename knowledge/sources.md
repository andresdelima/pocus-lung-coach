## Intellectual-property policy (set by the project author, 2026-11-19)

1. **Concepts only, in our own words.** The view cards paraphrase facts and techniques. No sentence is copied from any source. Names of signs and techniques (for example "bat sign", "TACA") are terminology and may be used.
2. **No third-party images, videos, figures or schematics in the portal, the repository, or anything sent to the AI model.** This applies to every source, open access or not. Example images for the model come only from the author's own de-identified pilot images (author decision, 2026-11-19). **Exception (author decision, 2026-11-22):** simulated reference images drawn by our own code (`scripts/make_reference_images.py`, saved in `app/static/reference/`) are shown in the portal and sent to the model as a labeled reference for each view. They contain no third-party content.
3. **Open-licensed sources (CC BY)** may be adapted with credit. Third-party figures inside them can carry their own restrictions and are treated as rule 2.
4. **All-rights-reserved sources** are used for paraphrased concepts only. The PDFs, their extracted text and our notes stay on this computer and are never committed (`knowledge/literature/` and `knowledge/notes/` are gitignored).
5. **Sources that restrict AI use are removed.** Flower L, et al. *Crit Care Clin* 2025 reserves rights "including for text and data mining, AI training", so it was removed from all cards, and its project copies (PDF, text, notes) were deleted on 2026-11-19 (author decision).
6. **Scope: image acquisition and interpretation only.** No needle procedures, nerve blocks or other interventions are taught, even when a source describes them (author decision, 2026-11-19).
7. **When in doubt, ask the author.** No assumptions.

Check with `.venv/bin/python scripts/ip_screen.py`. It looks for 7+ word runs shared between any card and any source PDF, quoted phrases, third-party media in the repo or the portal, and confirms the source material is gitignored. **Limitation:** web sources such as PC23 have no local text, so they can't be overlap-checked automatically. Their content was paraphrased twice (notes, then cards).
