## Purpose
Show lung motion in a single still image. This is the best input for judging sliding when no clip is available [L08 p3–4][B24 p5–6]. Use it whenever sliding is unclear in B-mode [PC23 §Lung Sliding using M-Mode (Optional)].

## Setup
- Start from a good card 01 view (bat sign, pleural line centered).
- Place the M-mode cursor **between the two ribs, through the pleural line**, not through a rib shadow. M-mode plots motion along that one line over time [B24 p5–6].
- **Looking for a lung point:** put the cursor exactly where sliding seems to start and stop [PC23 §Pneumothorax].
- Capture at least one full breath on the trace. If available, an ECG overlay helps show the lung pulse [B24 p5].

## Target image (must see)
1. **B-mode reference with cursor:** the top half of the screen shows the B-mode reference with the cursor through the pleura between two ribs.
2. **M-mode trace:** the M-mode trace shows the still chest wall above and the pleural line, with the pattern below it readable over at least one breath.

## Recommended settings
- **Depth:** same as card 01, start at about 4–6 cm, pleural line in the upper half, ideally the upper third. *(Author-approved starting value.)*
- **Sweep speed:** no fixed value; slow enough that at least one full breath fits on the trace.

## Common errors and fixes
| What you see | Coach says |
|---|---|
| Cursor sits in a rib shadow, trace below is black | "Move the cursor into the gap between the ribs, through the pleural line." |
| Trace too short to include a breath | "Slow the sweep speed or record longer so at least one breath is captured." |
| Pleural line not identifiable on the trace | "Go back to B-mode and confirm the bat sign first." [M21 p5] |

## Findings to teach (educational only)
- **Seashore sign:** horizontal lines (still chest wall) above, and a grainy, sand-like pattern below the pleural line. This pattern means lung sliding is present at this spot [L08 p3][B24 p6]. Memory aid: skin and fat are the sky, muscle is the sea, and the sliding lung is the beach [PC23 §Identify Lung Sliding].
- **Barcode (stratosphere) sign:** horizontal lines both above and below the pleural line, meaning no motion at this spot [L08 p4][B24 p6]. It is not specific to one cause: report it as a finding, not a conclusion [L08 p3][B24 p6].
- **Lung point:** the trace switches between seashore and barcode at the same spot as the patient breathes [L08 p3–4][B24 p4]. It can be hard to find [PC23 §Pneumothorax]. A lung point is a finding to report to the team right away.
- **Lung pulse:** small blips at the pleural line in time with the heartbeat, with no sliding. It shows the lung is touching the chest wall at that spot even though it isn't sliding [B24 p5].
- **Reading the trace:** seashore at this spot means the pleural layers touch here; keep checking the other points of the exam [PC23 §Pneumothorax].

## Quality checklist
- Landmarks: B-mode reference shows the bat sign and the cursor between the ribs.
- Orientation: cursor perpendicular through the pleural line.
- Depth: pleural line in the upper half (ideal: upper third), enough of the trace below it.
- Gain/focus: chest wall lines vs the pattern below the pleura clearly separable.
- Interpretability: at least one breath captured, so seashore vs barcode can be called.
